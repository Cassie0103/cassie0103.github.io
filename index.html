Capstone project

POS and Inventory Management for Sunsave Solar Energy Shop

Project Leader and Lead Programmer

We developed a Point-of-Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System to support daily operations across two Sunsave Solar Energy Shop branches. It helps staff manage product records, sales transactions, inventory by branch, procurement, supplier returns, reservations, and customer warranty claims.

The system is designed to help staff process transactions accurately, monitor stock by branch, trace products, and generate reports. As project leader and lead programmer, I led the team and took primary responsibility for programming the system.
